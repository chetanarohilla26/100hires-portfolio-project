## Source selection rule

Only recommendations that can be connected to a primary source are treated as source-backed recommendations.

The Stage 1 synthesis is used for pattern recognition, but individual recommendations are not presented as independently verified unless a source is attached.

## Note on dates

Where LinkedIn exposes relative dates such as "2y" in public page rendering, the post date was reconstructed from the LinkedIn activity identifier's timestamp and used only for the citation format required by the assignment. The exact source URL remains the primary verification reference.
