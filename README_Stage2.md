# Stage 2 — LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

**100Hires Portfolio Assignment**

**Author:** Chetana Rohilla

---

## Overview

Stage 2 builds upon the practitioner research completed in Stage 1.

Rather than summarizing content marketing advice, this stage converts the research into an actionable operating system that an early-stage B2B SaaS company could realistically adopt.

The deliverables synthesize recurring patterns across the researched practitioners while explicitly identifying areas of disagreement, evidence limitations, and original recommendations.

The objective is not to identify a universal LinkedIn formula.

The objective is to develop an evidence-informed framework that can be tested, measured, and iterated.

# Methodology

The recommendations in this repository were developed using the following process:

1. Review practitioner content from the Stage 1 research.
2. Identify recurring strategic themes.
3. Compare areas of agreement and disagreement.
4. Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
5. Synthesize practical recommendations.
6. Convert strategic ideas into repeatable operating procedures.
7. Design an execution framework that can be tested over time.

Where an idea represents my own synthesis rather than a direct recommendation from a practitioner, it is explicitly identified as an original framework.
